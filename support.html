When Reporting a Problem

To help us understand and resolve an issue more quickly, please include the following information when possible:

  • Your iPad model
  • Your iPadOS version
  • Your PageBuddy version
  • A short description of what happened
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, if applicable
  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if helpful
