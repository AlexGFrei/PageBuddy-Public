Reporting a Problem

If something isn't working as expected, including the following information can help us understand and resolve the issue more quickly.

  • Your iPad model
  • Your iPadOS version
  • Your PageBuddy version
  • A short description of the problem
  • Steps to reproduce it, if possible
  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if helpful
