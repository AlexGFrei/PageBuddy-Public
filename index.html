PageBuddy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

PageBuddy has no servers. PageBuddy does not operate its own server-side database, analytics infrastructure, advertising system, or tracking infrastructure.

Your PDF files remain where you put them. PageBuddy does not upload your PDF files to PageBuddy servers or require you to import them into a separate PageBuddy cloud library.

When iCloud synchronization is enabled, PageBuddy uses Apple's iCloud services, including CloudKit and iCloud Drive, to synchronize PageBuddy data between your devices.

1. About PageBuddy

PageBuddy ("PageBuddy", "we", "our", or "us") is an iPad application for reading and annotating PDF documents.

PageBuddy is designed to work with PDF files in the folders and locations chosen by the user. The app provides reading, annotation, document organization, and reading-progress features without requiring users to upload their PDF files to PageBuddy-operated servers.

2. Information PageBuddy Stores

PageBuddy stores information necessary to provide its reading, annotation, synchronization, and document-management features. Depending on the features you use and your iCloud settings, this information may be stored locally on your device and synchronized through Apple's iCloud services.

Book and document metadata

PageBuddy may store information associated with documents you add to or follow in the app, including information such as:

  • document title;
  • author information when available;
  • file location or path;
  • document identification information;
  • folder information.

Reading data

PageBuddy may store:

  • current page;
  • reading progress;
  • reading status;
  • reading sessions;
  • favorites and related document status.

Annotation data

PageBuddy stores information required to display and synchronize annotations created by you, including annotation metadata and drawing data created with Apple Pencil or other supported input methods.

Search history

PageBuddy may store the search queries you perform within the application so that search history can be provided and synchronized between your devices.

3. Your PDF Files

PageBuddy works with PDF files in the folders and locations you choose. The application does not require you to copy or upload your PDF files into a PageBuddy-controlled cloud library.

PageBuddy does not upload the contents of your PDF files to PageBuddy servers. PageBuddy has no servers that receive or store your PDF documents.

If a PDF is itself stored in a cloud service such as iCloud Drive, that service may independently synchronize the PDF according to its own functionality and settings. Such synchronization is not performed by PageBuddy.

4. Document Identification

PageBuddy may use a cryptographic identifier derived from the contents of a PDF file to associate the document with its PageBuddy data.

PageBuddy currently uses a SHA-256 hash of the PDF as part of this identification process. This allows PageBuddy to recognize the same document when it appears again in a folder followed by the application, without requiring the PDF itself to be uploaded to PageBuddy servers.

This mechanism also allows identical PDF files to share the same associated PageBuddy annotations and reading state.

5. Annotations and Original Files

Annotations created in PageBuddy are stored separately from the original PDF file.

Creating annotations in PageBuddy does not modify the original PDF. Your original document remains unchanged unless you explicitly choose to export annotations into it.

PageBuddy can export annotations into an existing PDF or create a new annotated copy while keeping the original PDF unchanged. Exporting annotations is an explicit user action.

6. iCloud Synchronization

When iCloud synchronization is available and enabled, PageBuddy uses Apple's iCloud services to synchronize PageBuddy data between devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

CloudKit

PageBuddy uses Apple's CloudKit service to synchronize structured application data.

This may include:

  • book and document metadata;
  • reading progress;
  • reading status;
  • reading sessions;
  • annotation metadata;
  • favorites and document status;
  • followed folder information;
  • search history.

This information is synchronized using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. PageBuddy does not operate or control the servers used to provide CloudKit.

iCloud Drive

PageBuddy also uses iCloud Drive to synchronize binary and file-based application data required by the app.

This may include:

  • annotation drawing files;
  • per-document reading state files.

These files are stored in PageBuddy's iCloud container and may be accessible through Apple's Files app and iCloud settings, depending on the user's device and configuration.

PageBuddy does not use a separate third-party synchronization service or its own cloud storage infrastructure.

iCloud services are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy and the user's Apple Account and iCloud settings.

7. Data Stored Locally

Some PageBuddy data is stored locally on your device. This may include cached document information, thumbnails, application settings, annotations, reading state, and other data required for the application's operation.

Data that is processed and remains only on your device is not transmitted to PageBuddy or to PageBuddy-operated servers.

8. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

PageBuddy does not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, tracking services, or data brokers.

PageBuddy does not use your documents, annotations, reading activity, search history, or other PageBuddy data for advertising, behavioral profiling, or targeted marketing.

PageBuddy does not sell your personal data.

9. Third-Party Services

PageBuddy uses Apple's system frameworks and services, including iCloud, CloudKit, and iCloud Drive, where applicable.

PageBuddy does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or user-data collection SDKs.

Apple's services are operated by Apple and are subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

10. Data Deletion

PageBuddy provides controls for deleting data stored by the application.

Long-press a book → Clear Annotations

Deletes the annotations associated with that book and resets its reading progress.

Settings → Data Management → Delete All Data

Permanently removes PageBuddy's stored books and document records, annotations, reading progress, reading sessions, search history, and app settings from the locations controlled by PageBuddy, including the corresponding PageBuddy iCloud data.

This action cannot be undone.

Deleting PageBuddy from your iPad is different from using Delete All Data.

If you delete the application without first using Delete All Data, data previously synchronized by PageBuddy through iCloud may remain in Apple's iCloud infrastructure and may become available again if PageBuddy is reinstalled and iCloud synchronization is enabled.

Local-only application data, such as cached thumbnails and local application settings, is removed by iOS when the application is deleted.

To completely remove PageBuddy's stored data, use Settings → Data Management → Delete All Data before deleting the application.

Deleting PageBuddy data does not delete your original PDF files. PageBuddy's data and your original documents are stored and managed separately.

11. Data Retention

PageBuddy retains local and synchronized application data for as long as it is needed to provide the application's features or until you delete it using the data-management controls described in this policy.

PageBuddy does not maintain an independent server-side database from which it retains copies of your PageBuddy data.

Data synchronized through Apple's iCloud services may remain in Apple's infrastructure according to Apple's systems, policies, and your Apple Account and iCloud settings until it is deleted through PageBuddy or the applicable Apple mechanisms.

12. Children's Privacy

PageBuddy does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

PageBuddy does not use advertising, tracking, or behavioral profiling.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through PageBuddy in a manner inconsistent with this policy, please contact us using the information below.

13. Your Privacy Choices

You control whether PageBuddy can use iCloud synchronization through your Apple Account and iCloud settings.

You can also manage PageBuddy's stored data directly within the application using the data-management features described above.

If you have a privacy-related question or need assistance regarding your PageBuddy data, you can contact us using the contact information below.
