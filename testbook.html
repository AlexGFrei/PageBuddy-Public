PageBuddy Test Book 1
PAGEBUDDY · HOW IT WORKS

Reading and writing belong together.

Traditional PDF applications often separate reading from editing. Before writing something down, the reader may need to find a markup tool, switch modes, select a pen, and then switch back to reading.

PageBuddy removes that interruption. The document remains the center of the experience while Apple Pencil is available for writing directly on the page.

“The best annotation interface is the one you do not have to think about.”

A reader should be able to underline an important sentence at the moment it becomes important, circle an unfamiliar concept, or write a short thought before it disappears.

The goal is not to turn reading into editing. The goal is to allow thinking to happen without interrupting reading.

PAGEBUDDY · YOUR FILES

Your PDFs stay where they are.

PageBuddy works with PDF files stored in folders selected by the user. It does not require importing an entire library into a separate application vault.

The original PDF remains unchanged while you read and annotate. PageBuddy stores annotation information separately from the original document.

Original PDF
remains unchanged.

Annotations
are stored separately and associated with the document.

Export
creates an annotated PDF when you want to share or save your annotations outside the app.

This approach makes it possible to preserve the original document while still allowing the reader to make the book their own.

PAGEBUDDY · REMEMBERED

Pick up where you left off.

Reading is rarely completed in a single session. A book may be opened for a few minutes in the morning, revisited later in the day, and returned to weeks afterward.

PageBuddy remembers information associated with the documents you read, including reading progress and annotations.

When the same document appears again in one of the folders tracked by the app, PageBuddy can recognize it and restore information associated with that document.

“A good reading tool remembers the book, not just the file.”

Reading progress and annotation data can also synchronize between compatible devices through Apple's iCloud services when available and enabled.

  4. Verify that annotations remain available.
